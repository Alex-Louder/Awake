The Clamshell Problem Solved

Engineered for developers, creators, and remote teams who refuse to let a closed hinge interrupt a single server, render, or download.

Architecture

The Clamshell Trap vs. The Awake! Protocol

macOS assumes that closing your laptop means you have stopped working. Awake! gives you back total control.

Default macOS Behavior
Disruptive
  • •
    Background tasks freeze: 4K video renders, Docker containers, database migrations, and large downloads halt immediately.
  • •
    USB bus power cut: External SSDs and audio interfaces abruptly disconnect, risking file corruption and audio glitches.
  • •
    Remote Desktop black screens: WindowServer kills the display framebuffer. Remote sessions via Sills, VNC, and RustDesk go dark.
  • •
    Audio blares in public: Podcasts, music, or videos keep playing if you lower the lid quickly in quiet rooms.
The Awake! Protocol
Seamless
  • •
    Uninterrupted compute: CPU and GPU continue operating at full capacity. Local servers and renders finish without stopping.
  • •
    Guaranteed USB bus power: External NVMe drives, USB hubs, and audio DACs (Focusrite, Apollo) stay continuously powered.
  • •
    Crystal-clear Remote Desktop: Screen brightness drops to zero to save battery, but GPU framebuffer streams at 60 FPS.
  • •
    Shh! Auto-Mute Guardian: Instantly pauses playback and mutes speakers/AirPods on lid shut. Restores volume on open.
Capabilities

Built for power users. Refined for everyone.

Engineered in pure native Swift. No Electron, no Chromium overhead, and no background daemons when idle.

Shh! Smart Audio & Media Curtain

Hooks directly into macOS CoreAudio and MediaRemote. Whether you listen via internal speakers, AirPods, or an external USB audio interface, closing the lid mutes sound instantly and pauses playback. It seamlessly restores your previous volume when you open the lid.

IOKit Clamshell Hook MRMediaRemoteSendCommand(kMRPause)
Internal Speakers • AirPods Max • Focusrite / Apollo DACs

No Dummy Plugs Needed

Awake! dims your physical display backlight to 0 via DisplayServices while maintaining the WindowServer framebuffer. Connect remotely via Sills, VNC, RustDesk, or AnyDesk without black screen errors.

Framebuffer Active (60 Hz)

Zero Daily Password Prompts

Awake! executes with minimum privileges. It sets a single rule in /etc/sudoers.d/awake scoped strictly to pmset -a disablesleep. The app itself never runs as root.

%admin ALL=(ALL) NOPASSWD: /usr/bin/pmset -a disablesleep *

~15 MB RAM • 0.0% Idle CPU

No periodic polling loops. Awake! relies purely on IOKit kernel event interrupts, leaving your CPU, RAM, and battery completely free for actual compilation and creative work.

Activity Monitor: 15.2 MB 0.0% CPU Idle
How It Operates

The 3-Step Hardware Pipeline

Transparent, clean Swift execution directly interfacing with macOS system frameworks.

STEP 01

1. IOKit Magnetic Sensor

Listens for physical clamshell state changes directly through AppleACPIPlatformExpert and IOPMrootDomain.

IOServiceAddInterestNotification(...)
STEP 02

2. Scoped pmset Assertion

Applies disablesleep 1 with zero password prompt, telling macOS power management to ignore the clamshell trigger.

/usr/bin/pmset -a disablesleep 1
STEP 03

3. Display & Audio Hooks

Dims screen backlight to 0 while preserving WindowServer, pauses active media, and mutes audio output.

DisplayServicesSetBrightness(0)

Apple Silicon MacBooks (M1–M4) run exceptionally cool at moderate loads.
